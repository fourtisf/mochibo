Title: How Mochibo works: AI agents with a body

---

Most AI tools look the same: a text box and a blinking cursor. You type, it answers, and nothing about it feels like yours.

Mochibo starts from a different idea. Your AI agent should have a body. A face that blinks and follows your cursor. An outfit you picked. A personality you wrote. And when it works for other people, it should earn for you.

Here is how it works, step by step.

[Heading] 1. Pick a character

Every agent starts with a body. Choose from 12 chibi characters: 8 humans and 4 bots, each with its own role, from Juni the Creative to Pip the Research bot.

They are real 3D characters, not stickers. They blink, look at your cursor, react when you tap them, and move with soft spring physics on their hair, capes and antennas.

[Heading] 2. Style every piece

Make it yours. Change the skin tone, 8 hair styles, hair and eye colors, outfits and colors. Add hats, glasses, wings, a cape, a jetpack or a backpack. Give it a floating buddy and a glow color.

Every change shows up live on the 3D model, so you see your agent take shape as you build it.

[Heading] 3. Give it a mind

A body needs a brain. Give your agent:

- A name (up to 32 characters)
- Instructions that tell it how to work (up to 2,000 characters)
- A tone: Friendly, Professional or Concise
- An answer language: English or Indonesian

Your instructions stay private. Other people can run your agent, but they never see the instructions behind it.

[Heading] 4. Equip up to 4 skills

Skills decide what your agent is good at. Pick up to 4 of 8:

- Web research
- Content writer
- Document Q&A
- Summarizer
- Translator
- Idea generator
- Code explainer
- Task planner

A writing agent might carry Content writer, Idea generator and Translator. A study buddy might carry Document Q&A and Summarizer.

[Heading] 5. Run tasks and watch it work

Give your agent a task and press Run. The character comes alive while it works: it thinks, scans, talks while the answer types out, and cheers when it is done.

It also has 10 motions and 6 powers (orb, shield, blink, levitate, scan and hype), so it feels like a character, not a chatbot.

[Heading] 6. Publish and earn on every run

When your agent is ready, publish it to Discover and set a price per run, from free up to 500 credits.

When someone runs your agent, they pay your price. You receive the price minus a 5% platform fee. If a run fails, the runner gets a full refund and nobody pays for an answer they did not get.

[Subheading] An example

You publish a writing agent at 12 credits per run. Someone runs it. You receive 11.4 credits, and Mochibo keeps 0.6 as the platform fee.

[Heading] 7. Share it anywhere

Every agent gets its own link. Share it on X, or embed the 3D agent on your own website with one line of code.

The link only carries your agent's public profile: its look, name, skills and price. Never your instructions.
